About the photo-analysis feature and what I hope to build next.

Hi! I'm Kaveri, and I'm in Grade 7. I created PlateCheck AI because I wanted to use technology to make learning about food groups more interesting. You can upload or take a photo of your food and see it displayed here. I wanted the app to go one step further and use AI to recognise the food in the picture and suggest what food groups could be added. To make that work, I need to connect the app to an AI API. This requires paid credits, and I wasn't able to get those credits because the cost was too high for my project. So the photo upload works, but the automatic AI analysis is not available yet. I hope that when I have the funds, I can continue developing this app so that people can take a photo of their meal and receive suggestions about food groups they could add. Food Pyramid: Questions & Answers

Quick answers visitors can explore during the exhibition.

1. What is a balanced diet?

A balanced diet includes a variety of foods that provide energy and nutrients the body needs, such as carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals, fibre and water.

2. Why are grains placed at the bottom of this pyramid?

Grains such as rice, wheat and oats are important sources of energy. Whole grains can also provide fibre and B vitamins.

3. Why should we eat fruits and vegetables?

They provide vitamins, minerals, fibre and other helpful plant compounds that support normal body functions and digestion.

4. What do protein foods do?

Protein provides amino acids that help the body grow, maintain tissues and repair them. Examples include beans, lentils, eggs, fish and meat.

5. Why do we need dairy or calcium-rich alternatives?

Milk, curd, cheese and suitable fortified alternatives can provide calcium and protein, which support bones and teeth.

6. Why should foods high in added sugar be limited?

They can add a lot of sugar without providing many essential nutrients. They can be enjoyed sometimes, but should not replace a variety of nourishing foods.

7. Why is water important?

Water helps regulate body temperature, transport substances and support many body processes.

8. Does everyone need exactly the same diet?

No. Food needs can differ with age, activity, health, culture and personal circumstances. The pyramid is a general learning guide, not a strict rule for every person.
